What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital asset that operates using blockchain technology.

What is XRP?

XRP is a digital asset associated with the XRP Ledger, a blockchain designed for fast transactions.

What is Market Cap?

Market capitalization is generally calculated by multiplying an asset's price by its circulating supply.
