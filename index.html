How to read crypto prices

A cryptocurrency price is the amount quoted for one unit of a digital asset. Market prices can change quickly across exchanges and trading pairs.

  • Price: the quoted value per coin or token.
  • 24-hour change: the percentage change over the previous 24 hours.
  • Trading volume: the value traded during a given period.
  • Market capitalisation: price multiplied by circulating supply.

Market risk reminder

Crypto assets are volatile. Prices can rise or fall rapidly, and some assets can lose most or all of their value.

Research an asset, understand transaction fees and never invest money you cannot afford to lose.

Crypto learning centre

Educational resources

What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a decentralised digital currency that uses a public blockchain to record transactions. Its price can be affected by demand, supply, liquidity and wider market conditions.

Understanding XRP

XRP is a digital asset associated with the XRP Ledger. Learn about its transaction system, market liquidity, risks and the difference between the asset and related companies.

Trading risk management

Before making a trade, set a budget, understand the order type, account for fees and decide in advance how much loss you can tolerate. Avoid decisions based only on online predictions.

Protect your crypto wallet

Never share your recovery phrase or private keys. Verify wallet addresses, use official applications and beware of promises of guaranteed profits or free giveaways.
